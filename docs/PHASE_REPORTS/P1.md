## Deviations from Plan (and why)
- **A4 Human Override**: The human selected to permit dynamic LLM rewrites of bullet text (relaxing strict author-only variants for those specific rewrites). Documented in `docs/DECISIONS.md` under D-004: dynamic rewrites will be clearly tagged, validated against hallucinations, and fully reversible in 1 click to author-written baselines.
- **Tectonic / XeTeX Glyphtounicode Compatibility**: `resume.tex` originally contained `\input{glyphtounicode}` and `\pdfgentounicode=1` which are pdfTeX-specific. Wrapped these macros in `\usepackage{iftex}\ifPDFTeX ... \fi` in `templates/resume.tex` to ensure clean dual-compatibility with both Tectonic (XeTeX) and pdfLaTeX.
